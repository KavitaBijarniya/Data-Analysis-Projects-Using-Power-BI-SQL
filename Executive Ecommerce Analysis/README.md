# 📊 Executive E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The Executive E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and marketing channel effectiveness.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics and enables users to explore data through interactive filters and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to help users monitor business performance by providing insights into:

* Sales and profit trends
* Customer performance
* Product performance
* Regional performance
* Marketing channel effectiveness
* Discount impact on profitability

---

## 📂 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Margin %
* Average Order Value (AOV)

#### Visualizations

* Monthly Sales and Profit Trend
* Orders by Category
* Orders by Region
* Orders by Marketing Channel
* KPI Comparison View

#### Filters

* Month
* Category
* Region
* Marketing Channel

---

### 2️⃣ Detailed Analysis

Provides deeper analysis of customer, product, and regional performance.

#### Visualizations

* Sales by Region and Marketing Channel
* Top Customers by Sales
* Top Customers by Profit
* Customer Order Analysis
* Discount Group Analysis
* Product Performance Table

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Interactive KPI Cards
* Multi-Page Dashboard Navigation

* Cross-Filtering Between Visuals
* Customer Performance Analysis

## 📊 Business Analysis Areas

### Customer Analysis

* Compare customer sales performance
* Identify top customers by sales
* Identify top customers by profit

### Product Analysis

* Evaluate product-level sales performance
* Compare profitability across products
* Analyze margin performance

### Regional Analysis

* Compare sales performance across regions
* Evaluate regional contribution to revenue

### Marketing Channel Analysis

* Compare sales generated by different marketing channels
* Evaluate channel performance across regions

### Discount Analysis

* Analyze sales and profit across discount groups
* Compare profitability by discount level
