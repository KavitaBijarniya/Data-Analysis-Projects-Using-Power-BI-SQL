# 📦 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard built using **Power BI, SQL, DAX, and Power Query**.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of supply chain operations, enabling business users to monitor supplier performance, manufacturing efficiency, inventory health, logistics, and sales performance from a single reporting solution.

The dashboard is divided into four business-focused pages:

- Supplier Performance
- Manufacturing Performance
- Inventory & Logistics
- Sales & Customer Insights

---

# Problem Statement

Supply chain teams often work with data spread across multiple systems, making it difficult to monitor operational performance and identify issues quickly. Without centralized reporting, stakeholders struggle to answer key business questions such as:

- Which suppliers are performing efficiently?
- Are production targets being achieved?
- Is inventory maintained at healthy levels?
- Which transportation methods incur the highest costs?
- Which locations and products generate the highest revenue?
- Where are operational bottlenecks affecting business performance?

The objective of this project was to build an interactive dashboard that consolidates supply chain data into meaningful KPIs and visualizations, enabling faster and more informed decision-making.

# Key Business Insights

## Supplier Performance

- Supplier 1 handled the highest order volume and generated the highest revenue contribution.
- Supplier lead times varied significantly, highlighting opportunities to improve procurement efficiency.
- Defect rates differed across suppliers, indicating the need for targeted quality improvement initiatives.
- Procurement costs varied across locations, suggesting opportunities for supplier and location cost optimization.

### Manufacturing Performance

- Production volumes were generally aligned with target volumes, while some SKUs showed noticeable gaps requiring operational attention.
- Production efficiency remained above 90%, indicating strong manufacturing performance.
- Higher defect rates were associated with certain suppliers, emphasizing the importance of supplier quality management.
- Manufacturing costs differed across product categories, supporting cost optimization decisions.

### Inventory & Logistics

- Shipping costs varied across carriers, providing opportunities to optimize logistics expenses.
- Transportation modes contributed differently to production movement, enabling comparisons of operational efficiency.
- The inventory dashboard highlighted both optimal and critical stock levels, helping identify products requiring replenishment.
- OTIF performance revealed opportunities to improve delivery reliability and customer service.

### Sales & Customer Insights

- Revenue distribution differed across locations, helping identify high-performing markets.
- Top-selling SKUs accounted for a significant share of product sales.
- Product categories contributed differently to revenue and profit, supporting product portfolio evaluation.
- Customer demographic analysis provided visibility into purchasing patterns across different customer groups.
