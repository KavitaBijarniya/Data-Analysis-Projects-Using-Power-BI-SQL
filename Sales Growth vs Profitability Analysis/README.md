# 📊 Sales Growth vs Profitability Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview

This project investigates a common business problem where sales are increasing but profitability is declining.

Using Power BI, I built an interactive dashboard to analyze revenue trends, discount strategies, and category performance to uncover the drivers behind margin compression.

The dashboard provides both high-level executive insights and detailed profitability analysis to support better business decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Despite strong revenue growth, the company started experiencing declining profit margins and overall losses by 2017.

### Key questions addressed in this analysis:

• Why is profitability decreasing despite increasing sales?

• Are high discounts reducing profit margins?

• Which product categories are generating losses?

• Do regional sales patterns affect profitability?

## 📊 Key KPIs Created

To evaluate business performance, the following KPIs were designed:

💰 Total Sales

📉 Total Profit

🏷️ Average Discount

📊 Profit Margin %

## 💡 Key Insights

### The analysis reveals several critical business insights:

📈 Sales increased significantly between 2014 and 2017, reaching $10.5M.

📉 Despite revenue growth, the company generated -$1.47M in total profit.

🪑 Furniture contributes ~48% of total sales but generates the largest losses.

🏷️ Profit margins declined sharply after 2016 due to increased discounting.

🌎 All regions show negative profit margins, indicating issues with pricing strategy and product mix rather than geographic performance.
