# 🛒 E-Commerce SQL Analysis Project
## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes an E-commerce order dataset to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, payment preferences, and operational issues such as cancellations and returns.

Using SQL analytical queries, the project converts raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making in an e-commerce environment.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

📈 Revenue trends

🏆 Top-performing product categories

💎 High-value customers

💳 Payment behavior

⚙️ Operational inefficiencies

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to use SQL-based data analysis to answer key business questions that an e-commerce manager would typically ask.

Key questions explored:

📅 How does revenue change month-to-month?

🛍️ Which product categories generate the most revenue?

🌍 Which cities have the highest order cancellation rate?

👤 Who are the most valuable customers?

💳 What payment methods do customers prefer?

🔄 Which product categories have high return rates?

By answering these questions, businesses can make data-driven decisions related to:

• Marketing strategy

• Operational efficiency

• Customer retention

## 🔍 Key Insights
### 📈 Revenue Trends

• Monthly revenue analysis revealed fluctuations throughout the year, indicating seasonal purchasing patterns that can guide marketing campaigns and promotional timing.

### 🛍️ Category Performance

• A small number of categories contribute a significant portion of total revenue, highlighting high-performing product segments that deserve strategic focus.

### 💎 Customer Value

• A small group of customers accounts for a large share of total revenue, demonstrating the importance of loyalty programs and targeted retention strategies.

### ⚙️ Operational Issues

• Certain cities show higher cancellation rates, which may indicate delivery challenges, logistics inefficiencies, or customer experience issues.

### 🔄 Product Returns

• Some product categories experience higher return rates, suggesting potential issues with product quality, sizing, or product descriptions.

### 💳 Payment Behavior

• UPI emerged as the most commonly used payment method, indicating a strong preference for digital payment options among customers.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
### 🔧 Improve High-Return Categories

Investigate categories with high return rates to improve:

• Product quality

• Product descriptions

• Size guidance

### 🎯 Target High-Value Customers

Introduce:

• Loyalty programs

• Exclusive offers

• Personalized marketing campaigns

### 🚚 Optimize Logistics in High-Cancellation Cities

Improve:

• Delivery performance

• Customer communication

• Logistics coordination

### 📢 Focus Marketing on High-Revenue Categories

• Allocate more marketing resources to top-performing product categories to maximize revenue growth.

### ⚡ Enhance Digital Payment Experience

Since digital payments dominate, invest in:

• Faster checkout experience

• Reliable payment gateways

• Seamless mobile payment flows
