# 💳 Financial Transaction Fraud Detection Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing financial transaction data to identify fraud patterns, high-risk segments, and financial impact.
An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to simulate how financial institutions monitor and investigate fraudulent activities.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Detect fraud patterns across transactions
* Identify high-risk customers, devices, and locations
* Analyze fraud impact in terms of both **volume and monetary loss**
* Enable dynamic exploration using interactive visuals

* Fraud Rate: **2.98%**
* Fraudulent Transactions: **179**
* Total Fraud Loss: **2.59M**
* Total Transaction Value: **84.85M**

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 Fraud Overview & Transaction Patterns

* Fraud Rate by Device Used
* Fraud Rate by Transaction Type
* Fraud Distribution by Merchant Category (Treemap)
* Fraud Trend Over Time
* Dynamic Metric Toggle:

  * Fraud Count
  * Fraud Rate
  * Fraud Amount

---

### 🔹 Fraud Risk & Customer Analysis

* High-Risk Customers by Fraud Rate
* Fraud Volume vs Fraud Loss (Scatter Plot)
* Geographic Distribution of Fraud
* Fraud Distribution by Channel & Device
* Top 10 Customers with Highest Fraud Loss

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* ATM and Mobile channels show higher fraud risk compared to other devices
* Fraud is concentrated within specific merchant categories such as Electronics and Travel
* A small percentage of customers contribute to a large share of fraud losses
* Fraud activity fluctuates over time, indicating possible behavioral or seasonal patterns
